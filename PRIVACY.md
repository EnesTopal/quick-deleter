# Privacy Policy — QuickDeleter

_Last updated: 2026-10-18_

QuickDeleter is an offline file manager.

- **No data collection.** The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It has no
  analytics, no advertising and no network access.
- **Storage access.** The "All files access" permission is used only to list, preview, move, rename
  and delete files that you choose on your device. Files never leave your device.
- **Undo trash.** Deleted files are kept for a few seconds in the hidden folder
  `.QuickDeleterTrash` on your internal storage so that the deletion can be undone. They are removed
  permanently after that, or the next time the app starts.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/EnesTopal/quick-deleter/issues.
